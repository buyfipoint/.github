## What is Buyfipoint?

Buyfipoint is an e-commerce platform designed around **fidelity-based purchasing**. Instead of traditional payment-only flows, customers earn and spend loyalty points when they buy products — creating a reward cycle that keeps them coming back.

The platform serves **retail businesses** (both online stores and physical shops) that want to offer a loyalty program integrated directly into their purchasing experience.

## Why Buyfipoint?

Most loyalty systems are bolted on as an afterthought — a separate card, a separate app, a separate experience. Buyfipoint makes fidelity **the core of the buying process**, not a side feature. This means:

- **For customers**: Earn points on every purchase, redeem them seamlessly, and access promotions, discounts, and special offers — all in one place.
- **For businesses**: Manage products, providers, pricing, promotions, and customer analytics from a single admin platform. No need to juggle multiple tools.

## Key features

- **Fidelity points system** — Customers earn and redeem points on purchases
- **Product management** — Categories, variants, and flexible pricing
- **Provider management** — Track and manage your suppliers
- **Order management** — Promotions, discounts, bundles, up-sell, and cross-sell
- **Customer analytics** — Reporting and insights on purchasing behavior
- **Multi-store support** — Run multiple shops from a single platform
- **User management** — Internal team members and customer accounts
